## 2. The 4 Pillars of the Method

```
                   Global DEV Acceleration Framework
                                  │
       ┌──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────┐
       ▼                          ▼                          ▼
1. Magnetic Positioning    2. Technical English        3. Interview Blueprint
- ATS Resume Architecture  - Cambridge CELTA Method    - 500h+ Real Footage
- Inbound US Recruiter Flow- Architecture Trade-offs   - Live Coding & Design
```

### Pillar 1: Magnetic Positioning & ATS Resume Architecture
Optimization of LinkedIn and GitHub profiles for algorithmic search engines used by US recruiters, eliminating blind application fatigue.

### Pillar 2: High-Impact Technical English (Cambridge CELTA)
Precision lexical structures for defending architectural trade-offs, managing technical debt, and leading high-stakes engineering discussions under pressure.

### Pillar 3: Real Interview Blueprint (+500 Hours Database)
Comprehensive video breakdown of actual live coding, distributed system design, and STAR behavioral interviews across Backend, Mobile (iOS/Android), QA/SDET, DevOps, and Data.

### Pillar 4: Direct B2B Contracting & Global Tax Engineering
Master Services Agreement (MSA) structuring, international invoice handling, and legal corporate setup.
