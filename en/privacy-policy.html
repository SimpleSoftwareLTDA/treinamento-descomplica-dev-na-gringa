Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Last updated: August 31, 2026. Simple Software LTDA is committed to data transparency, information security, and the complete privacy of your personal data.

1. Data Controller Identification

The data controller responsible for the processing of personal data collected through this website and marketing campaigns is SIMPLE SOFTWARE LTDA, founded by international software engineer and educator Robson Cassiano.

For any inquiries regarding this policy or to exercise your individual data privacy rights, contact us at: contato@robsoncassiano.software.

2. Personal Data We Collect

We only collect information necessary to deliver educational services and resources:

  • Lead & Form Submissions (LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms, Guides & Newsletters): Full name, professional email address, job title, and phone/WhatsApp number (when voluntarily provided).
  • Browsing & Telemetry Data: IP address, browser type, visited pages, and aggregated interaction telemetry (processed anonymously via Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity).
  • Transaction & Enrollment Data: Handled with bank-grade encryption by our checkout partner Hotmart. We do not store credit card numbers on our servers.

3. Purpose of Data Processing

Your data is processed strictly for the following purposes:

  • Providing access to complimentary educational tools (7 Steps Playbook, Google Gemini Notebook, and salary simulators);
  • Delivering course curriculum updates, new interview recording notifications, and program announcements;
  • Providing direct student mentorship and technical interview preparation support;
  • Maintaining technical security and preventing unauthorized access or fraud;
  • Complying with applicable legal and statutory obligations.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data in compliance with GDPR (Art. 6) and LGPD (Art. 7):

  • User Consent: Provided explicitly when submitting forms or interacting with our campaigns;
  • Contractual Performance: Necessary to provide mentoring and coursework access to enrolled students;
  • Legitimate Interest: To maintain platform performance, security, and relevant educational communications.

5. Third-Party Data Sharing

Simple Software LTDA never sells or rents your personal data. Data is shared only with trusted technological infrastructure partners:

  • Hotmart: Payment processing, invoice generation, and student portal access;
  • Cloud Infrastructure Providers: Cloudflare edge security and TLS 1.3 encrypted servers;
  • Analytics & Ad Networks: LinkedIn Ads, Google Analytics, and Microsoft Clarity using anonymized identifiers.

6. Your Data Subject Rights

Under GDPR and LGPD, you have the right at any time to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you;
  • Request immediate rectification of inaccurate or outdated information;
  • Request complete erasure (deletion) of your data from our systems;
  • Unsubscribe from email communications at any time with one click.
